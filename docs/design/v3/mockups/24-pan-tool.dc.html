Rules
Directions are on the screen, along the ground. "Up" is away from the viewer whatever the camera's azimuth, so the pad matches Shift + arrows.
Limits are the V2 limits. The target stays inside the site boundary plus 40 m and under the roof cap. Reaching one stops the movement; there is no bounce.
The tool is not remembered. A new session starts in Orbit; the pad is always there. 3D only; the 2D map already pans by dragging.
Every role may pan. It changes the camera only. The wall has no input, so no rail, pad or hint.
Reduced motion. A step jumps; a held arrow still moves, without the easing tail.
Keyboard. The pad is a labelled group of real buttons ("Pan north-east"); Space or Enter steps, and holding repeats.
