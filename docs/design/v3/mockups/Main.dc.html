Glow on every border flattens the hierarchy and competes with the model. Colour alone never carries severity.
Neon edge: 1 px accent + 14 px soft glow, on the active tab, selected button, selected row and the open panel only. Glass panels are ~90 % opaque; blur only on small floating overlays, off under reduced transparency and in the Standard and Low tiers.
