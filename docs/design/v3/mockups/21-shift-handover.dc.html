Rules
Who. Operators and supervisors hand over; technicians for their categories (Facilities, Environment). Viewers see no handover.
What it stores. Outgoing and incoming user, notes, the ids of the open and acknowledged incidents at that moment, created and accepted times. A new table; no incident changes.
Approval. The incoming operator's acceptance is enough by default; a supervisor's approval is an open question (ADR-0024).
History. The latest handover is read at sign-in; older ones are searchable later.
