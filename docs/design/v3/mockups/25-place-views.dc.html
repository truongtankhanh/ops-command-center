Ways in and out
Enter: double-click a building, Open plan in the summary card, Show in plan in an asset or incident inspector, or a place in the search.
Leave: Esc, the breadcrumb (Campus), or double-click empty ground. Switching Plan to Shell shows the building closed again.
The view moves only because the operator asked: a new alarm never opens a plan. Selecting an incident in the feed flies to its building as before; the plan opens only from Open plan, double-click or Show in plan.
Plan or shell
Plan is the cut-away, with walls kept low so devices are never hidden. Shell is the closed building from frame 08 with the level highlighted and the heat overlay available.
Roof and plant on the roof (cooling towers, air handlers) belong to the Roof level of the same building.

25f What the plan needsSmall, additive data on top of ADR-0023.
Rooms and walls
A level gets a list of rooms: code, name, kind (hall, lab, corridor, wet area, plant, office) and an outline in metres inside the building.
Walls and fixed furniture are drawn from the outlines and a small list of props per room (racks, benches, beds). No CAD import.
A device gets plan_x and plan_y in the level; without them it keeps the footprint dot. Both are optional.
Where it comes from
Authored by hand for the demo campus (five places) as seed data, in the repo, like the site plan.
Real sites need an editor or an import; that is not in V3.5 and not designed here.
25g Roles, loading and failureMoving the camera is not an action on the site.
Rules
Every role sees every plan; a viewer cannot act on a device, as in frame 11.
A technician sees all rooms but the actions follow their categories.
Plans load with the level, on demand and cached; while loading the footprint shows. A failed load shows the footprint and says "Plan unavailable".
Reduced motion: the camera cuts to the plan instead of flying.
A wall shows plans only when a person has opened one; an unattended wall never does.
